Export is a build. The timeline is split into chapters, and each chapter is rendered on its own and cached.

When you change something inside one chapter, only that chapter is rendered again. The rest comes straight from the cache, and the final file is stitched together without re-encoding the unchanged parts.

The renderer uses the hardware video engines on the chip, so a long four k sixty video takes minutes, not hours. It also checks loudness at the end and reports it, so you know the audio is ready for upload.

You can export a full video, a short clip for social media, or just the audio. Each preset sets resolution, frame rate, and bitrate for you.
