Now we record. Press the record button and the app starts three separate tracks, one for the screen, one for the camera, and one for the microphone.

Each track is written as a fragmented file. If the machine crashes or the battery dies, everything up to the last second is still on disk, and the project opens normally afterwards.

While you talk, you can press a key to drop a marker. Use a marker for a good line, a mistake, or a spot where you want a diagram to appear.

If you stumble over a sentence, just say it again. The editor notices that the second attempt is almost the same as the first one, and it keeps the better take.

When you are done, press stop, and the rough cut is ready within a few seconds.
