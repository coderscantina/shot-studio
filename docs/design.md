# Design

This document owns the visual rules of the app. The [mockup](mockup/README.md) shows the seven screens: Board, Script, Record, Cut, Edit in three workspaces, Product demo, Ship. It is a copy of the [mockup canvas](https://claude.ai/artifact/P6kQUAKTd96uCBaHTURQsa), where the screens are designed.

## Defaults

- **Theme**: dark only in the first release. Tokens are named by role (surface, raised, text, muted, accent, danger) so a light theme can be added later without touching screens.
- **Surfaces**: panels are separated by shade, not borders.
- **Accent**: lime `#C8F25A` for actions and data. Red only for recording and cuts.
- **Lane colors**: each kind of edit keeps one hue in lanes, tags and their icons: zoom blue `#5AA9FF`, b-roll violet `#B69CFF`, highlight lime, cuts red. No other hues. The transcript marks effects in the same hues (2026-11-08, from Mike's milestone 13 request): a zoom or an overlay underlines its words, a highlight washes them lime, a sound sits as a neutral chip at its word, and a chip with the effect's icon starts each one, filled for a person's effect and outlined for one from the style rules.
- **Type**: Geist, 15 px base. Geist Mono only for timecodes, durations, code and file names. Labels and values such as "Word pop" or "24%" stay in Geist.
- **Corners**: 8 to 14 px.
- **Components**: `gpui-component`, restyled with these tokens. Custom elements (timeline, waveform, transcript, preview surface) are built on raw gpui and use the same tokens.
- **Window**: minimum 1280x800.
- **Screenshots**: evidence is captured at 1600x1000 in dark.
- **Keyboard first**: every review action has a single-key shortcut, shown next to the button. In Edit, Backspace cuts the selected words; wherever Backspace removes, cuts or deletes something, forward Delete does the same, and hints show both keys (2026-11-08). In Text, ← → are a word cursor: without a selection they pick the word at the playhead, they step over cut words and on across takes and chapters, and after a cut the next word is selected with the playhead on the seam; ⇧Space plays 1.5 s on each side of it or of a picked cut, plain Space stays play and pause in Edit (2026-11-17, from the council on Mike's "clumsy" request, the agent's choice until Mike settles it). Tab accepts and Esc rejects a proposal only while it has focus (the caret is inside it or the card is selected), like ghost text in a code editor; otherwise Tab moves focus. Esc closes the topmost thing first: popover, then selection, then proposal. A timeline, list or transcript is one tab stop with arrow keys inside.
- **What is worth a look** (2026-11-18, milestone 17, [0040](decisions/0040-the-cut-proposes-itself.md)): the nav is Record, Edit, Ship, and the transcript is the one place a proposed cut is reviewed. Beside the chapter title, a raised chip with a lime dot says "N to look at" with its key N; it is gone at zero. N goes to the next item and plays it: 1.5 s around a restart the rules cut, with its chip picked, a second around an unsure finding, with its words selected and no floating toolbar, or the whole beat. K keeps what plays, C cuts, and the answer moves on to the next item. On a beat K and C both say it is fine as proposed, which is no pick: a later take that scores better still plays it. N, K, C and A act only in Text, and K and C answer the item N went to only while the playhead is still on what N played of it, else the item at the playhead. A beat with other attempts starts with a raised chip in Geist Mono, "2 of 3", or "1+3 of 3" when sentences of two attempts play, with a lime dot while it is still to look at. A plays the beat's next attempt and picks it: from sentences of two attempts the first attempt whole, and after the last attempt the proposed sentences again. ⇧A opens the takes side by side on that beat, and ⌘↩ comes back. The inspector's first section says what the item is, then its keys, then the beat's attempts with their scores, the playing ones lit.
- **Script editor** (2026-11-16, milestone 16, the agent's choice until Mike settles [0038](decisions/0038-script-editor-from-druck.md)): the Script screen's document pane is the editor, at most 720 px wide and centred. The title is 26 px and a chapter 19 px, both semibold in strong text; a beat is text color with a muted bullet in the margin, the lines under it body color. Markers (`#`, `- `, a link's brackets and target) show muted only on the caret's line or inside the link, and hang in the margin so the words never move. A link's label is lime and underlined. A misspelled word gets a red underline, as macOS draws it, and the line `Script::parse` refuses a red tint with its message under it in red: both are errors, like the screen's other error text. The lines a selected proposal changes get a faint lime band. Proposal cards stand above the editor.
- **Script suggestions and the agent pane** (2026-11-16, the agent's choice for [the bar for writing a script](technical-briefing.md#the-bar-for-writing-a-script), until Mike settles it): suggestions stand above the editor in one scrolling area of at most 60% of the pane. A suggestion that spans chapters shows one card per chapter, named by the chapter and "2 of 6, accepted together"; its words wrap as one text, struck words muted red with a red line, inserted words lime. Hook options stand side by side in sunk cards: "Hook A" and the spoken length in mono, then the full text, a beat in text color and its script in body color. The selected option gets the lime border and a lime Use this hook with Tab; Reject all (Esc) and Another set sit under the options. In the agent pane every run is one exchange, in order: the request as a raised bubble on the right, at most 85% wide; while it runs, the steps with a lime dot and Stop; once it ends, one muted line with a chevron ("Read 3 pages, proposed 4 changes") that opens the steps; the answer as selectable Markdown on the pane with a quiet Copy under it. A stopped run says Stopped in muted text, a failed one its error in red.
- **Time**: every timecode is in edited time, unless Untrimmed is on. All views of the same moment come from one cut list, so a word, block or proposal shows the same time everywhere.

## Settled rules

Rules Mike settles, with the date and the rejected variants and why each lost. The `design-variants` skill adds to this list.

- **Icons** (2026-11-07): the [Hugeicons](https://hugeicons.com) free set (MIT, `@hugeicons/core-free-icons`), stroke rounded at 1.5 px, colored with the text they sit beside, or with their lane color when they mark a kind of edit. 18 px in buttons, nav and lists, 14 px inside tags. Replaces the one-off inline SVGs of the first mockup.
- **Edit workspaces** (2026-11-07): the Edit screen has three workspaces over the same panes, switched in the title bar or with ⌥1 to ⌥3. Text: chapters, transcript as a document, preview with a selection inspector, overview bar. Picture: the preview fills the stage (1040 px wide at 1600x1000, smaller down to the 1280x800 minimum), floating panels stay inside it, caption style applies to the whole video, layout picker (1 to 4), on-screen layers, three rolling transcript lines, overview bar. Timeline: preview and change list on top, lanes below. In every workspace the transport and the Edited/Untrimmed switch sit under the preview, so switching workspaces never moves them. The transcript scrolls, and the selection toolbar is anchored to the selection with 8 px clear of other content. Auto cuts under 1 s show in the transcript as a faint 2 px seam without a label; longer cuts, your own and Claude's keep the red length chip. Pane headers are the drag handles, with a grip on hover. A menu next to the switcher lists your saved workspaces (⌥4 and up), sets the workspace each format opens in (Opinion opens in Text), and saves or resets a layout; panes resize by their edges and move by their titles. Text and Picture scored 8, the menu 8. Rejected: picking one fixed layout, from round 1's "Combined approach, allow to alter own workspace to match habits". Timeline lanes, one per kind: Layout, Zoom, B-roll, Highlight, Words (spoken words placed by time, cuts as red seams with their length, a hovered cut opens to show the removed words), Voice waveform, Music; lime playhead; a selection spans every lane with its H, Z and Backspace toolbar below. Scored 8, then 9 for the whole workspace. Rejected: the refined lanes (Effects lane shared by zoom, b-roll and highlight, white playhead, ruler ticks, Restore popover, ducking curve), "Keep round 2".
- **Agent proposals in the editor** (2026-11-07): shown inline where they apply, as struck and inserted words with the time saved, and Accept (Tab), Reject (Esc) and Another take. Away from the text, the card says where it applies and offers "Show at" with its timecode (G), the text keeps a 64ch measure, the time saved is muted ("saves 1.9 s"), and a lime spark marks it on the overview bar (9). The branch behind it stays out of sight. In the editor a proposal only removes or restores recorded words; it never invents speech, and caption text fixes go to Caption fixes instead. Rejected: the `cut.kdl` diff with a Merge button, scored 4, "Not user centered".
- **Lanes for styles and sound** (2026-11-07, effects grill): no new hues. The B-roll lane becomes Overlays, violet, for b-roll, cards, callouts, pops, lower thirds and the chapter band. A neutral Sound lane for SFX sits next to Music, which shows its cues as blocks. A transition is a mark on the chapter edge, not a lane. Rejected: one new hue for sound, and a design-variants round before deciding.
- **Whole-video overview** (2026-11-07): one bar for the full length, with chapter blocks, cut ticks, a frame around what the main pane shows, and the playhead. Text and Picture show it along the bottom; Timeline shows it above its lanes. Rejected: the chapter filmstrip with zoom and b-roll ribbons (6, no note), and the overview bar above lanes (7, "More polished, easier to use", kept as the top of the Timeline workspace).
